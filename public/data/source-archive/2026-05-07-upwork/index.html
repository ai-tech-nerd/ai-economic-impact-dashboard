Upwork

Date May 7, 2026
Jobs Affected ~151 (24-25%)
Category Product / Engineering / Cross-functional
Type layoff

Details

CEO Hayden Brown memo: 'Two pizza teams are dead. AI means smaller, differently resourced teams in product and engineering can make a bigger impact than ever.' On LinkedIn: 'AI is fundamentally reinventing work and we are building Upwork to be at the center of that -- the platform that powers the AI-enabled, human+agent labor market of the future.' Upwork rethought every function 'from scratch -- scrutinising what it takes to run each part of the business using both people and technology.' One-time expenses $16-23M for severance. Affects ~151 of ~630 employees.

Upwork Lays Off 25% Of Its Workforce, Says AI Will Lead To Smaller Teams

Published: 2026-05-08

"Tech layoffs are coming in thick and fast in the age of AI. Upwork CEO Hayden Brown announced on May 7, 2026, that the company would be cutting approximately a quarter of its workforce. The message, shared with employees and later published on Upwork's website, frames the decision as both a response to near-term economic headwinds and a deliberate structural bet on AI-powered, leaner teams."
