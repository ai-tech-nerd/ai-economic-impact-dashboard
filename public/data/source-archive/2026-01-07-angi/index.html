Date Jan 7, 2026
Jobs Affected 350 (~12.5%)
Category Customer service / Sales / Marketing

Details

SEC filing stated cuts made 'to reduce operating expenses and optimize the organizational structure in support of long-term growth and in light of AI-driven efficiency improvements.'
