ClickUp

Date May 21, 2026
Jobs Affected ~220 (22% of ~1,000)
Category Mixed / Company-wide (Engineering, PM, Design)
Type layoff

Details

Founder/CEO Zeb Evans on X: 'Today we reduced headcount by 22%. The business is the strongest it's ever been... the way to operate at the highest level of productivity is changing, and to win the future, ClickUp needs to change with it.' Restructuring around what Evans calls 'the 100x org' -- 100x output through AI. Key quotes: 'the great engineers, the ones who can orchestrate, architect, and review, are becoming 100x engineers. They're not writing code. They're directing agents that write code'; 'the bottlenecks are (1) orchestration -- telling AI what to do, and (2) reviewing -- what AI did. Everything is leapfrogged and no longer needed'; 'You must create enough disruption so that old systems are deprecated entirely. If there's any definition for AI native that's what it is.' Company now runs ~3,000 internal AI agents (3:1 agent-to-employee ratio per Fortune). Introducing $1 million cash/year salary bands. Severance packages provided. ClickUp valued at $4B in 2021.
