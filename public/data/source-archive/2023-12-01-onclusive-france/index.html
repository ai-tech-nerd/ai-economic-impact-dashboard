Onclusive, formerly known as Kantar, is reducing its teams to replace them with AI

Published: 2023-12-01

“At the end of 2023, the employees of Onclusive, a French company based in La Défense and a key player in media monitoring in France, became the actors of the first redundancy plan in France caused by the irruption of artificial intelligence (AI) in the world of work: 209 people will leave or have already left the company, which had up to 383 positions, made redundant to be replaced by AI-powered computers.”
