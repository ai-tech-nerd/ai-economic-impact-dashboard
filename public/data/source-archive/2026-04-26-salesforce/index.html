Salesforce Job Creation

Date Apr 26, 2026
Jobs Created 1,000 new graduates and interns
Category Engineering / Product / Sales (AI-focused on Agentforce + Headless360)
Type job creation

Details

CEO Marc Benioff on X: 'I'm locked on, @DavidSacks! We're hiring 1,000 new grads & interns right now to ride the AI exponential. You are right they said AI would kill entry-level jobs. Meanwhile these grads & interns are building it -- powering Agentforce & Headless 360 at Salesforce.' Salesforce launched a new Builder Program within its university recruitment initiative (Futureforce) to fast-track recent graduates into engineering, product, and sales roles focused on its AI agent platform Agentforce. Company says it has hired over 10,000 professionals through Futureforce to date. Comes despite Salesforce's own February 2026 cuts of nearly 1,000 roles (already in tracker). Benioff also said he is hiring more salespeople because 'demand than ever' even while not hiring more software developers or service agents in FY26 due to AI agent capabilities.
