Published: 2026-10-01

“BMW AG plans to use artificial intelligence to eliminate about 20% of its senior management roles by the middle of next year, as the German luxury carmaker looks to cut costs, simplify its management structure and improve profitability, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday.
