HoneyBook lays off 14% of workforce in AI-driven restructuring

Published: 2026-10-08

“HoneyBook, a company that develops business management tools for small businesses in the U.S., is laying off approximately 14% of its 255-strong workforce. The layoffs affect around 30 to 35 employees, the majority of whom are based in Israel. The company is estimated to have hundreds of millions of dollars in cash reserves.”
