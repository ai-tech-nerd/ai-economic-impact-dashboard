Sprout Social cuts 20% of workforce in restructuring plan

Published: 2026-07-15

“Sprout Social began notifying employees on Tuesday about a workforce reduction that will eliminate approximately 260 positions, or 20% of its staff. The company’s board approved the restructuring plan on July 8, 2026. The social media management company said the plan aims to streamline its organizational structure and align costs with strategic priorities, including ongoing investments in AI-powered social intelligence.”
