Visa is cutting 7% of employees in efficiency push as AI reshapes work

Published: 2026-07-28

“Visa, which runs the world’s largest payments network, plans to cut about 7% of its workforce as CEO Ryan McInerney moves to streamline the company and invest more in growth areas, according to a memo confirmed by CNBC. The company plans to eliminate roughly 2,600 positions, mostly in its technology and product operations, according to the memo.”
