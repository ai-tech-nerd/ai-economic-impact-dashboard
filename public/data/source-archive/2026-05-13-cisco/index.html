Cisco

Date May 13, 2026
Jobs Affected ~4,000 (<5%)
Category Mixed / Company-wide
Type layoff

Details

CEO Chuck Robbins blog post: 'We are making strategic investments in our employees' use of AI across the company.' Restructuring to redirect investments toward AI chips, fiber optics, and security. SEC filing: up to $1 billion in restructuring costs (mostly severance), $450M this quarter, remainder in FY27. Notifications began May 14. TechCrunch headline: 'Cisco cuts nearly 4,000 jobs to spend more on AI, reports record quarterly revenue.' Cisco offering one year of access to Cisco U courses including AI certifications. Robbins separately said he 'did not want AI to become the excuse' but company's own communications tie the cuts to funding AI investments. Separate event from Aug 2025 (~221) and 2024 (~6,000) cuts.

Cisco cuts nearly 4,000 jobs to spend more on AI, reports 'record quarterly revenue'

Published: 2026-05-14

"Technology giant Cisco is cutting nearly 4,000 jobs, or around 5% of its workforce, despite reporting better-than-expected profit and revenue in its fiscal third quarter. The networking equipment maker said it is reducing its headcount in order to change its 'cost structure' and invest in AI and cybersecurity."
