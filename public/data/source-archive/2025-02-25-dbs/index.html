DBS Group Job Never Created

Date Feb 25, 2025
Jobs Affected ~4,000 (contract/temp roles not renewed over three years)
Category Contract and temporary staff across 19 markets
Type job never created

Details

CEO Piyush Gupta said DBS will reduce around 4,000 of its roughly 8,000-9,000 contract and temporary staff over three years as AI takes on their roles, through non-renewal ("natural attrition") rather than layoffs. Permanent staff are not affected; DBS also expects to create about 1,000 AI-related roles.

DBS CEO sees job cuts of 4,000 temp staff as AI replaces roles

Published: 2025-02-25

“DBS Group Holdings Ltd plans to cut around 4,000 of its contract and temporary staff workforce over the next three years as artificial intelligence (AI) increasingly takes on roles carried out by human beings. Southeast Asia’s largest lender has approximately 8,000 to 9,000 of such staff, according to chief executive officer Piyush Gupta, replying to a query from Bloomberg News.”
