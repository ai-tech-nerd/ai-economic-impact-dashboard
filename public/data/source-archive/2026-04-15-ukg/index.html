UKG (Ultimate Kronos Group)

Date Apr 15, 2026
Jobs Affected ~950 (6% of ~16,000)
Category Multiple functions and regions / CX / Implementation / Operations
Type layoff

Details

UKG official statement: 'As part of UKG's ongoing transformation, we are restructuring parts of our workforce, impacting approximately 950 employees worldwide, or 6% of our workforce. These actions span multiple functions and regions and reflect rapidly evolving market shifts -- including changes in technology driven by AI, customer expectations, and how software companies compete -- and will better align our structure, skills, and investments for the future.' CEO Jennifer Morgan (former SAP co-CEO, joined 2024) is openly transforming UKG into an 'AI-first company.' ~600 employees exited immediately; ~350 on transition through August 31. Notification by email April 15, 2026. Heavy hits to Sunrise and Weston, Florida offices. Second major cut in 18 months (2,200 cut in late 2024).

UKG announces 950 layoffs, affecting a large number of workers in South Florida

Published: 2026-04-17

"As many as 950 employees of Ultimate Kronos Group (UKG), many of them from offices in Sunrise and Weston, were notified on Wednesday that their jobs would be eliminated in an apparent global restructuring. Layoff notifications were emailed on April 15 by the human resources and workforce management giant that was known as Ultimate Software until it merged with Kronos in 2020. One affected employee posted the layoff notification on the social media platform Reddit."
