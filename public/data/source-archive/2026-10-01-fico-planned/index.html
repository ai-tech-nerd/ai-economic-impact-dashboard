Fair Isaac Corporation Form 8-K, Item 2.05: Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities

Published: 2026-10-06

“On October 1, 2026, management of Fair Isaac Corporation (the “Company”) committed to a plan of workforce reduction by reducing the number of layers in the organization, simplifying the operating structure, optimizing processes and tools, and integrating AI-driven product development. This plan involves the elimination of approximately 15% of positions across the Company.”
