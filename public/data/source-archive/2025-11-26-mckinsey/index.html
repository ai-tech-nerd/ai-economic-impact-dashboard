McKinsey cuts about 200 tech jobs, shifts more roles to AI

Published: 2025-11-26

“[SINGAPORE] McKinsey & Co cut about 200 global tech jobs in the past week as the consulting firm joins rivals in using artificial intelligence to automate some positions.
