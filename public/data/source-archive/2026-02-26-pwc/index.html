PwC

Date Feb 26, 2026
Jobs Affected ~600
Category Executive Assistants / Recruiters / Talent Acquisition / Support Staff (incl. ~170 in Buenos Aires, plus Tampa)
Type layoff

Details

PwC US spokesperson to Bloomberg: 'modernising and simplifying how we work. Just as we help our clients do every day, we're becoming more digital, more efficient and better able to reinvest in growth and align more closely with firm and market needs.' Bloomberg reporting (sourced from people familiar): 'PwC's February cuts were influenced by the firm's push to adopt AI, both in terms of gearing up for automation and cutting costs.' Cuts wiped out the entire TID (Talent Acquisition) department from the top down per Going Concern. PwC has invested $1B in OpenAI/ChatGPT and rolled out AI tools across 100,000+ employees. Firmwide Talent Acquisition Leader Margaret Burke had warned in November 2025 of 'transformation' coming. The February cut included EAs whose work (scheduling, expenses, travel booking, inbox triage) is being absorbed by AI tools.
