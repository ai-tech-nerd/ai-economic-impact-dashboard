Meta — America's Workforce Academy Job Creation

Date Jun 10, 2026
Jobs Created Unspecified; guaranteed job offers for all graduates at Meta AI data center construction sites ($115M first-year investment)
Category Skilled trades: electricians, fiber technicians, welders, plumbers, mechanics, data center construction/operations technicians
Type job creation

Details

Meta launched a $115M free 5-week training program with guaranteed employment at its AI data center construction sites, supporting its ~$600B US data center buildout through 2028. Pilots in Louisiana, Ohio, Indiana, Texas.
Source ↗ fortune.com

A 5-week course and a guaranteed job: Meta commits $115 million to solve the skilled-trades shortage stalling its AI build-out

Published: 2026-06-10

“Trade jobs are the future, and Meta knows it. The company is launching an initiative called America’s Workforce Academy to train data center technicians in partnership with commercial real estate giant CBRE; Associated Builders and Contractors, a construction trade association; and civil rights organization National Urban League. Meta is committing $115 million this year to provide free training and a guaranteed job upon the program’s completion.”
