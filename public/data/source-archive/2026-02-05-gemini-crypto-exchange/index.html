Gemini (crypto exchange)

Date Feb 5, 2026
Jobs Affected ~30% of workforce
Category Mixed / Operations
Type layoff

Details

Workforce reduced by roughly 30% since start of 2026 through multiple layoff rounds. Company deploying AI tools to increase productivity. Winklevoss-led exchange lost over $500M in 2025. As of March 1, company employed about 445 people.

Crypto exchange Gemini plans to lay off up to 200 staff, exit Europe and Australia/h2>
Published: 2026-02-05

“Feb 5 (Reuters) - Gemini Space Station (GEMI.O), the cryptocurrency exchange founded by Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss, said on Thursday it plans to cut up to 200 jobs globally and focus operations in the U.S. and Singapore, as part of a broader cost-cutting effort. The planned layoffs, which involve about a quarter of its workforce, will affect staff in Europe, the U.S. and Singapore, the company said.”
