Jobs Affected ~312 (26% of ~1,181)

Details

C3.ai board approved restructuring plan cutting 26% of global workforce under new CEO Stephen Ehikian. SEC filing: 'intended to materially improve its operating efficiency and position the company for success.' Sales and marketing hardest hit at 36%. Expected to save ~$60M/year with $10-12M in restructuring charges. Also cutting ~30% of non-employee costs. Revenue had declined sharply from $98.8M to $53.3M quarter-over-quarter.
