Oracle

Date Mar 31, 2026
Jobs Affected 20,000-30,000 (~18%)
Category Cloud / Revenue & Health Sciences / SaaS & Virtual Operations / NetSuite India / Engineering
Type layoff

Details

Oracle began mass layoffs via 6 a.m. termination emails signed 'Oracle Leadership' with no prior notice from HR or managers. TD Cowen estimates 20,000-30,000 affected (~18% of workforce). Hardest-hit: Revenue & Health Sciences and SaaS operations (~30% each), plus NetSuite India Development Centre. Cuts expected to free up $8-10B in cash flow to fund $156B AI data center buildout. Oracle disclosed $2.1B restructuring plan in March 2026 SEC filing. Bloomberg reported March 5 that cuts would target 'roles the company expects it will need less of due to AI.'
