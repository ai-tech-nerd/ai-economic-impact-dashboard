Snap Inc.

Date Apr 15, 2026
Jobs Affected ~1,000 (~16% of ~5,261)
Category Mixed / Company-wide
Type layoff

Details

CEO Evan Spiegel memo via SEC filing: 'While these changes are necessary to realize Snap's long-term potential, we believe that rapid advancements in artificial intelligence enable our teams to reduce repetitive work, increase velocity, and better support our community, partners, and advertisers.' Described it as a 'crucible moment' demanding 'a new way of working that is faster and more efficient.' Said small squads using AI tools drove 'meaningful progress' on Snapchat+, ad platform, and Snap Lite. Also closing 300+ open roles. Expects $500M+ annualized savings by H2 2026. Activist investor Irenic Capital had recommended cutting ~1,000 roles citing AI. US employees receive 4 months severance.
