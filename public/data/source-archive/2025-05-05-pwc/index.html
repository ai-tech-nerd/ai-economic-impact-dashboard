PwC

Date May 5, 2025
Jobs Affected ~1,500 (~2% of US workforce)
Category Assurance / Tax / Products & Technology
Type layoff

Details

Deanna Byrne (Assurance lead at PwC) internal communication: 'We are proceeding with a reduction in workforce across various sections of our US operations -- this includes Assurance, Tax, and parts of our legacy Products & Technology sector.' Senior partner Paul Griggs (US North America operations): 'We are in an era of reinvention, with many companies aiming to transform their businesses through data, AI, and other emerging technologies while also fostering greater trust with stakeholders and maintaining the integrity of the capital markets.' Context: PwC made a $1 billion investment in OpenAI/ChatGPT and rolled out the platform to 100,000+ employees in 2024. Three-year ChatGPT partnership announced in 2023. PwC also cut campus recruiting in light of the workforce reduction. Forbes noted: 'AI is transforming the service workforce.' Separate from the February 2026 cuts of ~600 EAs/recruiters.
