Mews lays off 15% of staff in pivot to 'AI-native' future

Published: 2026-07-08

“Hospitality software provider Mews is cutting 15% of its 1,350-person staff in what it is calling a ‘proactive decision,’ a company spokesperson told Hotel Dive. Affected employees were notified Tuesday, per the Mews spokesperson. Customer-facing roles were largely not impacted by the cuts, which represent the company’s largest restructure since the pandemic.”
