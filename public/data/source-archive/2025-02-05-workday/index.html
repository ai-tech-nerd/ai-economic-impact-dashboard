Workday to cut 1,750 jobs in AI push

Published: 2025-02-05

“Feb 5 (Reuters) - Workday (WDAY.O) said on Wednesday it will cut around 1,750 jobs, or 8.5% of its current workforce, as the human capital management firm invests heavily in artificial intelligence to counter a softer macroeconomic environment. Shares of the California-based company jumped over 4% in premarket trading.”
