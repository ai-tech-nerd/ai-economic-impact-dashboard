PayPal will cut about 2,500 jobs, or 9% of global workforce

Published: 2024-01-30

“PayPal will cut 9% of its global workforce, or about 2,500 jobs, CEO Alex Chriss wrote Tuesday in an internal letter to employees, which was viewed by CNBC. The cuts will affect both existing roles and job listings that PayPal had planned to hire for, and will take place over the course of the year, Chriss wrote. Affected employees will be notified by the end of the week.”
