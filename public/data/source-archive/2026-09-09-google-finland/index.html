Google deepens its commitment to Finland with a €13 billion investment in AI infrastructure

Published: 2026-09-09

“Today's announcement, our largest single investment in Europe, will provide a significant boost to the Finnish economy. During the initial construction phase in 2027 and 2028, over 37,000 jobs nationwide will be supported and will contribute €3.6 billion annually to Finland's GDP. Once fully operational, the new facilities will support thousands of permanent jobs, ranging from engineers to security and catering staff.”
